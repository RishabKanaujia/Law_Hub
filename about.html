About Me

Hi, I’m Rishab Kanaujia — a law student at
The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda

Law became a natural area of interest as I began exploring how legal rules shape society and influence everyday life. My exposure to technology further enhanced my ability to analyze information, think critically, and present complex ideas in a structured manner.

As I progressed in my legal journey, I had the opportunity to observe the law beyond textbooks—through courtrooms, legal practice, and interactions with people seeking solutions to real-world problems. These experiences reinforced an important realization: law is not merely a collection of statutes and precedents, but a living system that continuously evolves alongside society and human relationships.

Every experience, whether in academic study, legal research, internships, or practical exposure, has contributed to a deeper understanding of how legal principles operate in practice. At the same time, the legal profession remains a field of constant learning, where every case, courtroom, and conversation offers a new perspective.

This platform is my digital notebook—a space where I share simplified Bare Acts, case law summaries, legal articles, and insights drawn from academic learning, courtrooms, internships, and practical legal exposure. My goal is to make legal knowledge simpler, practical, and easier to understand while continuing to learn, explore, and grow alongside the legal community.

“The life of the law has not been logic; it has been experience.”
